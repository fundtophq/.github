# fundtop

The top of open source: a weekly leaderboard where fans chip in for the open-source projects they use.

- Back any listed project with $5 or more. The biggest total at Monday 00:00 UTC takes #1, then the board resets.
- Every $5: $4.10 to the project's maintainers (90% after card fees), $0.44 card fees, $0.46 fundtop.
- Every payout is public.

**Maintainers:** projects with a GitHub Sponsors or Open Collective page will be listed at launch. Claim yours to receive payouts, or ask to be left off by [opening an issue](https://github.com/fundtophq/.github/issues/new) or emailing fundtop.dev@gmail.com. Being listed never means a project endorses fundtop.

**Status:** preview. Payments are not live. See the preview at [fundtophq.github.io](https://fundtophq.github.io).
